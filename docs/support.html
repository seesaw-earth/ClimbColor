ClimbColor Support

ClimbColor is a free, open-source iPhone app for highlighting same-color climbing holds from a wall photo.

Quick help

  • Take a wall photo or choose one or more photos from your library.
  • Wait for on-device hold detection to finish.
  • Tap a hold to highlight holds with a similar color.
  • Use the edit controls to add or remove holds from the selected route.
  • Use Save/Share to export an annotated image.

Privacy

Detection and color analysis run on device. The developer does not operate an account, analytics, advertising, or image-upload service for ClimbColor.
